About

Hi, I’m Paolo

One hire. Three roles.

For clients, I deliver the coordinated output of a strategist, a content creator, and an operations assistant, from one person, without the overhead of hiring three.

The instinct behind those results, knowing what actually moves a number, now runs through full marketing operations: building the pipeline, writing the campaign, publishing the content, and reporting what worked.

I earned my degree in Marketing Management from National University in Bulacan, and since 2024 I have taken on freelance and contract work across CRM systems, ad campaigns, and content calendars. In October 2026 I became a Certified Marketing Management Specialist (CMMS) through CPACE Philippines, adding to the CDMP I earned in 2024.

Something about me

I’m an INFJ-A, “the Advocate.”

INFJ is one of the rarest personality types, often estimated at just 1–2% of people. Advocates lead with empathy and intuition, picking up on what people need before they say it, and hold themselves to high standards. The Assertive (-A) side stays calm under pressure and trusts its own judgment, so feedback turns into fuel instead of stress.
